# EatEasy

Practical restaurant booking app in Portuguese. Preserve the eatEasy wordmark, green accent, PHP routes and form field names. Poppins throughout; 14px body, 12–13px metadata, 24–32px page headings. Flat surfaces with 8px corners, restrained borders and no drop shadows. Desktop sidebar becomes a compact horizontal navigation on mobile. Light and dark modes share semantic tokens. Keep keyboard focus visible, native form validation and reduced motion. Sample restaurants are explicitly demo data, not real listings.
